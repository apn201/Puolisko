Can one face be scored per side?

The YouCam Skin AI API scores the whole face. This page tests two ways to get a score per half, on your own photos. Every analysis costs 12 units. Nothing is saved.

  1. Mask split (what the app uses): one call, then split each concern's detection mask at the midline.
  2. Mirror composite (the fallback): build a face from each half plus its reflection, two calls, compare whole-face scores.
  3. Repeatability: load 2 to 4 photos taken a minute apart. The spread of L − R between them is the measurement noise.
